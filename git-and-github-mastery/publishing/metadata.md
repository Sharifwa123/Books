3. **Right to redistribute** — granted by the text licence, under its conditions (attribution, share-alike).
4. **Right to modify** — granted by the text licence, under its conditions.
5. **Right to use commercially** — [TO CONFIRM against verified licence text and publisher intent: CC BY-SA generally permits commercial use with attribution and share-alike; if the publisher wants to prohibit commercial use, BY-SA is the wrong choice (that would be BY-NC-SA)].
