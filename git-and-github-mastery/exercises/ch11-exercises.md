# Chapter 11 exercises — Centralised vs Distributed

Attempt each exercise before you open `solutions/ch11-solutions.md`.

## Level 1 — Guided

**1.1 Draw them.** On paper, draw the centralised and distributed designs for three people. Label what each person holds.

## Level 2 — Partially guided

**2.1 Which design?** For each statement say "centralised", "distributed" or "either": (a) "I can read the whole history with no network." (b) "There is one official copy by design." (c) "If the server is lost, every copy is still a full backup." (d) "My computer holds only one version of the files."

## Level 3 — Independent

**3.1** Explain to a friend why "distributed" does not mean "there is no shared server". Use the phrase "by convention".

## Level 4 — Professional scenario

**4.1** A school club of eight students and one teacher will keep the website source under version control. Students often work on trains and at home with poor connections. Recommend a design and justify it with three points from section 11.4. Name one thing the group must agree on.

## Level 5 — Troubleshooting

**5.1** A learner says: "My repository is just the latest files; the history is on GitHub." Identify the misunderstanding and correct it.

**5.2** A team's shared server is down. Half the team says work must stop; the other half says it need not. Who is right, in a distributed design, and what can and cannot continue?
