# Chapter 37 exercises — Account and Profile

Attempt each exercise before you open `solutions/ch37-solutions.md`. **These exercises need no live account.** They are planning exercises; check each answer against GitHub's current documentation.

## Level 1 — Guided

**1.1 Decide first.** On paper, write the email address and the username that you would use for a new account, and one sentence saying why for each.

**1.2 Your Git identity.** Run `git config --get user.name` and `git config --get user.email` (Chapter 14<!--ref:config-->). Which of these would appear in a public commit?

## Level 2 — Partially guided

**2.1 Public or private?** For each item, say whether you would put it on a public profile: a photo of your face; your favourite programming language; your home town; your employer's name; your phone number.

**2.2 The address.** If your username were `ada-bakes` and your repository `sunrise-bakery`, what would the repository's address look like (Chapter 36<!--ref:whatgh-->)?

## Level 3 — Independent

**3.1 Email planning.** You have a private email address and a work email address. Which would you set as `user.email` for public projects, and why? What is the risk of the other choice?

**3.2 A profile plan.** List the three repositories (real or imagined) that you would pin and why.

## Level 4 — Professional scenario

**4.1 Personal and work.** A new job asks you to contribute to their repositories. Write a short plan for keeping personal projects and work projects apart (accounts, emails, and identities in Git).

## Level 5 — Troubleshooting

**5.1** A learner made public commits using a private email address, and now wants it removed from history. Explain why this is hard, and what to do before the first commit next time.

**5.2** A learner's commits do not show their profile picture on the platform. Give one possible reason related to this chapter.

**5.3** A recruiter comments on a quiet contribution graph. How should the learner think about it?
