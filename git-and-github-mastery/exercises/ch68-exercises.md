# Chapter 68 exercises — Organizations and Enterprise Concepts

Attempt each exercise before you open `solutions/ch68-solutions.md`. These exercises are on paper; none needs an organization.

## Level 1 — Guided

**1.1 Three accounts.** Name the three kinds of GitHub account and say which of them can be signed in to.

**1.2 Roles in order.** List the five repository roles of an organization from least to most access.

## Level 2 — Partially guided

**2.1 Which role?** Choose the least role that fits: (a) a designer who only comments on issues, (b) a developer who pushes code, (c) a lead who manages the repository but must not delete it.

**2.2 Base permission.** An organization sets the base permission to Read. Does that change an outside collaborator's access? Does it change a member who was granted Write on one repository?

## Level 3 — Independent

**3.1 A structure.** Design the teams for a small company with a website team, a mobile team and a documentation group that everyone can read. Say which repository role each team gets on each repository.

**3.2 Owners.** Explain in three sentences why an organization needs more than one owner.

## Level 4 — Professional scenario

**4.1 A move.** A team's repositories live under the personal account of its founder. Write a short proposal to move to an organization: the benefits from this chapter and the decisions you would make first (section 68.5).

## Level 5 — Troubleshooting

**5.1** A member who left still has access through a deploy key. Which warning of the chapter applies, and what should have been done about the key?

**5.2** Your colleague reads a plans list in an old book and quotes a price. What do you tell them?
