# Chapter 6 solutions

## Level 1
**1.1** depends on your account. If 2FA is off, consider switching it on and saving recovery codes the same day.

**1.2** Length usually matters more than character-class rules: a long passphrase has many more possible combinations than a short password with a capital and a number, and it is easier to remember. It should be unique to that service.

## Level 2
**2.1** (a) authentication; check the password (and the account name). (b) authorization; ask an owner or administrator to grant the permission. (c) authentication (the second factor); use the phone or a recovery code. (d) authorization (visibility): only people who have been given access can see it.

**2.2** Any consistent analogy: for example at a library, showing your card is authentication and the card's borrowing rights (children's section only) are authorization.

## Level 3
**3.1** A good answer: "You give everyone an open padlock (which only you can open). To prove it is you, someone hands you a puzzle that needs the matching key to solve. You solve it at home with the key and send back only the answer. The key never travels."

## Level 4
**4.1** One plan: each person has their own account (no shared password); permissions are per person or per team, and contractors get access only to the projects they work on; a token or key is issued for each tool and each has the minimum permissions; access is reviewed regularly; when a contractor leaves, remove their access and revoke their tokens and keys the same day.

## Level 5
**5.1** Authorization. The colleague's identity was accepted. Someone who owns or administers the resource must grant the needed permission.

**5.2** Suggested order: (1) use any other signed-in device or a backup second factor if one exists; (2) look for the service's account-recovery process and follow it exactly (expect proof of identity and possibly a waiting period); (3) once in, generate new recovery codes and store them safely; (4) add a second factor, such as a security key. Next time: save recovery codes and register a backup factor on the day 2FA is enabled.
